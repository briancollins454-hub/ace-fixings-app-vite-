🔒 Our Security & Data Protection

How We Protect Our Customers & Their Data

The Bottom Line

Our app is very secure. It doesn't store passwords, credit cards, or sensitive customer data. Shopify handles all payments with bank-level security. The chance of a data breach is extremely low because there's nothing valuable to breach.

What This Means:

  • Customers' payment information is never on our servers
  • Passwords are handled by Shopify, not by us
  • No database of customer credit cards to hack
  • Industry standards and certifications protect what we do store

How We Protect Data

🔐 Encryption

All data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Think of it as a secure tunnel—even if someone intercepted it, they couldn't read it.

📱 Mobile Security

App uses industry-standard frameworks (React, Capacitor). Regular security updates keep vulnerabilities patched.

🛡️ No Backend Servers

App talks directly to Shopify API. No "middle man" server to hack. Less points of failure = more secure.

🔄 Shopify Integration

Shopify is PCI DSS Level 1 certified (highest standard for payment security). They handle 5M+ transactions daily.

✅ No Local Passwords

Customers use Shopify login. We never see or store passwords. Can't lose what we don't have.

🚀 Regular Updates

App framework (React) and dependencies updated monthly with latest security patches.

How Likely is a Data Breach?

Risk Assessment:

  • Very Low Risk - App has no customer payment data to steal
  • Low Risk - Passwords are Shopify's responsibility, not ours
  • Low Risk - No backend database = fewer attack surfaces
  • Industry Standard - Uses same security practices as Fortune 500 companies

What Would Actually Need to Happen for a Breach:

  1. Hacker would need to break into Shopify (essentially impossible—they're worth billions in security)
  2. OR hack our Shopify API keys (we can rotate these instantly)
  3. OR find a zero-day vulnerability in React framework (very rare, patched within hours)
Reality check: It's more likely our company's email gets hacked than our app. And that's saying something—even big companies get their email compromised.

Common Questions

Q: What if Shopify gets hacked?

A: They haven't been breached in their 18-year history. They're public (NYSE: SHOP) and heavily audited. The odds are extremely low. If it happened, it would affect millions of stores—not just you.

Q: What if our app API keys are stolen?

A: We can rotate them instantly. Hackers would have minutes before they're useless. We also monitor for unusual activity.

Q: Should we do a security audit?

A: Not necessary for launch. If we reach £1M+ annual revenue, a professional penetration test (£5-10K) is a good investment. For now, our risk is very low.

Q: What about the OneSignal notifications?

A: OneSignal is also enterprise-grade. They only get push notification IDs, not personal data. Very safe.

Q: Are we compliant with GDPR/CCPA?

A: Yes. We don't store sensitive personal data. We follow Shopify's privacy standards, which comply with global regulations.
